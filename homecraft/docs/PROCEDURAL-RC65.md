# RC65 – village house visual upgrade
The three existing village homes use procedural wall panels around real recesses for windows and doors, instead of solid boxes with overlays. The building footprint, NPC routes and ownership remain unchanged. The new exterior includes inset glass with a procedural reflection gradient, window frames, crossbars, shutters, sills, a hinge-origin door mesh, a two-slope roof, ridge caps, fascia, gutters, downpipes and chimney. Window emissive lighting follows the existing day/night cycle. Existing flower boxes and wall lamps are retained. The player-designed house builder is not replaced. The doors of these NPC facades are decorative; the player-owned house door interaction remains the existing system. Tests cover geometry partition, roof normal orientation and day/night material response. Visual and Android acceptance remain pending.
