# Realism II

Local PBR ground, plaster, asphalt and tile textures; high/ultra reflection environment; gradient sky, sun and stars; wet asphalt and wind-dependent water; facade detail, window glow, steering and emissive brake lights. Visual only, no game state migrations. Reflections are environment approximations (not planar scene reflections); headlamps emit visually but do not project a beam yet. Current procedural art is not scanned photorealism. Device benchmarks and visual QA remain necessary.
