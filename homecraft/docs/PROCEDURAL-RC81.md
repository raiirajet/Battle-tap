# RC81 – natural human silhouette
The user-approved visual target is a semi-realistic modern life-sim human, not a segmented toy. Realistic player and NPC avatars now use one generated weighted surface with skin, shirt and trousers material regions. The old rigid torso, sleeve and trouser meshes are hidden only after successful binding. The same bone weights and facial morphs drive garment deformation across shoulders, elbows and knees. The garment shell has local thickness, waist/elbow/knee folds, seam shading and sleeve/shorts boundaries. Shoe meshes, profile options, belt, accessories and existing animation clips remain. Head proportions and eye scale are reduced, short hair is redesigned with tapered hairline and curved locks. Runtime fallback remains articulated if the worker fails, with periodic retry while active. No external GLB dependency or save migration. This is a procedural approximation of the design reference, not a reproduction of a photorealistic sculpt; clothing cut boundaries and loose coats may need artist refinement. Verify on-device silhouette, all outfit combinations, performance and motion before release.
