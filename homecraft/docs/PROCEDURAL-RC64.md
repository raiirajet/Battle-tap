# RC64 – environment visual quality pass
Live park trees and bushes now add folded individual leaf/narrow needle geometry to crown clusters at near and middle LOD, with coloured vein shading, a subtle foliage lighting approximation, and offline bark/birch materials. No external textures. Medium can show the near foliage LOD within 10 metres. Meadow ground palette uses layered tileable value noise. Existing orthogonal village road graph is rendered as a single non-overlapping asphalt union, with edge shading, a low junction apron and bevelled paving shoulders interrupted at multi-edge junctions. This preserves simulation coordinates; detailed accessible curb ramps and physical puddle reflections are not implemented. Paving has its own procedural PBR maps. Added tests for geometry, texture differences and junction area. Actual visual and mobile performance approval pending.
