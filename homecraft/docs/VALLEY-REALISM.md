# Valley realism — first integrated pass

Procedural offline Three.js models: articulated human and pet models, distinct cars with rotating wheels, shaped roofs and facades, layered instanced crowns, ground/asphalt textures and filmic tone mapping. No save schema, access, collision or economy changes. Existing house planner remains the source of playable houses. Art direction is semi-realistic mobile, not scanned AAA art. Geometry tests are provided; actual phone frame rate, visual inspection, APK and advanced art assets still require verification.
