# HomeCraft Valley RC29–40 — integrated development release
Version 1.0.0-rc.40. All changes are local/offline.
- RC29: more detailed facial anatomy, articulated body variants.
- RC30: five hairstyles, three body types, clothing, shoes, accessories, rotatable dashboard 3D preview; old profiles migrate via defaults.
- RC31: bone-driven rigid mesh hierarchy, running outdoors, jump animation only (no vertical collision or obstacle crossing). Existing owner-only movement and interaction checks remain.
- RC32: cockpit with seats, dashboard, instruments and steering wheel; exterior shell is hidden in cockpit view for visibility.
- RC33/34: detail layers for supported furniture, kitchen and bathroom catalog assets on imported houses; no changes to floor plan or custom GLB models.
- RC35/36: curbs, crosswalk markings, drains, background mountains and instanced lakeshore rocks; decorative, not collidable.
- RC37: planar water reflections on high/ultra near lake, throttled to ~8 Hz, actual player car spotlights on high/ultra.
- RC38: market and workshop window displays, existing NPC routines and commerce preserved; public buildings are not newly enterable.
- RC39: orbit, first-person and raised camera on foot, chase/orbit/cockpit when driving.
- RC40: render pacing and quality tiers; geometry/logic tests and browser interaction smoke tests; physical-device FPS, upgrade and final visual QA remain outstanding until performed on a device.
Not a photorealistic scanned-asset pack. No new licensed third-party assets or account services.
