# RC72–74 atmospheric refinement
RC72: bounded NPC contact shadows, day/cloud/twilight-dependent hemisphere and direct lighting, world-plane shadow target snapping. RC73: layered cirrus and rim lighting in the existing procedural sky shader. RC74: deterministic two-layer normal waves and animated roughness on wet-road pools, correction of seasonal snow changes and rain wrap. Lake reflection remains the existing quality-gated reflector. Wet-road ripples are shader approximations, not independent geometric puddle reflections. All textures are generated offline. Unit tests cover lighting and water buffers; shader and Android validation remain required.
