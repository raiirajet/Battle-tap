# Crownfall 3D Asset Pack 0.3 — semi-realistic direction
507 primary models + 184 separate vertex-cluster LOD models (heroes, units, completed buildings). 512px embedded hero maps, 256px other base models, 128px LOD maps. 18 standalone 1024/2048px tileable material master maps (six materials, basecolor/normal/ORM; NOT unwrapped atlases). New face/eye/hair/hand geometry, cloth folds and cape animation, weighted glTF skin for humanoid body with joint-transition weights around elbows/knees; rigid props attached to sockets. This is procedural geometry, not hand-sculpted final art. Skin weights are approximate and segmented surfaces remain; no IK, motion capture, professionally retopologized continuous body, final UV atlas, art approval, or proof of triangle budgets/performance. LODs are generated by clustering and not yet engine-switched. Build via Actions; game integration is separate. Structural tests check hashes, joint/accessor relationships, LOD counts, resolution and catalogue IDs. No claim of Khronos validation or device acceptance.
